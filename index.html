Common Questions

What you're probably wondering

Do I have to generate my own leads?
No. Appointments are pre-set and credit pre-approved. No cold calling, telemarketing, or door-to-door prospecting is required.
Is this hourly or salary-based?
No. This is a 100% commission-only opportunity. It fits best for people already comfortable with performance-based compensation.
Is there any guaranteed pay?
Yes. There is a guaranteed $25 pitch commission for each completed qualified presentation, regardless of whether a sale is made.
What types of sales backgrounds qualify?
Home improvement, automotive, financial services, high-ticket retail, and commission-only contract sales are all strong fits.
What's the typical commission per deal?
Average commission per closed sale runs $600–$700, with deal sizes ranging from $299 to $10,000+ depending on product and conversation.
How quickly will I hear back?
Submit your qualification and we'll review your background. Qualified candidates typically hear back within one business day.
