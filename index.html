FAQ

Questions People Usually Ask

Do I have to generate my own leads?

No. The opportunity is structured around pre-set appointments and qualified conversations rather than cold prospecting.

Is this salary or hourly?

No. This is a commission-based opportunity intended for people who are comfortable performing in that environment.

What kind of experience matters most?

Prior sales experience matters most, especially when it involved self-direction, presentation skill, and closing ability.

Is training required?

Yes. If selected, candidates must attend a 2.5-day training class. Hotel accommodations are provided as part of that process.
