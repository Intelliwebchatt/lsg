01 / WHY HOME

The platform shift is already here

The music can live everywhere. The artist needs one place to stand.

Spotify says it removed more than 75 million spammy tracks in a year. Its current verification process also looks for public markers of an identifiable artist, including linked social accounts, concert dates, and merchandise.

A website cannot guarantee verification or stop a platform takedown. It can give your identity, catalog, credits, links, merchandise, and contact information one consistent public home. It reduces dependence on any single company without pretending platform rules no longer matter.

Spotify AI policy ↗ Verification criteria ↗
Reality check

AI is not automatically the problem.

Spotify says human artists using AI responsibly can remain eligible when they present themselves authentically. Profiles primarily representing AI personas are not currently eligible for verification review. LSG Artist Homes is an identity foundation, not a loophole.
